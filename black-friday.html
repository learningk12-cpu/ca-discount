Black Friday Canada — buying guide

Five things worth knowing before you buy this week.

  • Check the was-price on the store's own page. A "70% off" claim only means something if the regular price is real. Every deal here was checked that way — never from a flyer or another deals site.
  • Don't wait for Friday. Most Canadian Black Friday sales started early — Best Buy Canada's runs November 19 to December 3 — and the best items sell out before Friday.
  • Some prices vary by store. Leon's prices, for example, can differ by location, so confirm the price for your store before you go.
  • Cyber Monday is November 30. Many Black Friday deals carry over, and some stores add online-only offers that day — worth one more check on Monday morning.
  • Order early for delivery. Delivery slots fill up fast in Black Friday week, and some items already show delivery after December 9.

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Cyber Monday 2026 is Monday, November 30. Many Black Friday deals continue into Cyber Monday, and some stores add online-only offers that day.
