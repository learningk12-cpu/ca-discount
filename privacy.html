Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026. CA Discount ("we", "us") runs cadiscount.ca. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it, in plain language.

Information you send us

If you contact us through the contact form or by email, we receive the name, email address and message you send. We use that information only to read and reply to your message. We do not sell it, share it, or add it to any mailing list.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes basic technical information when you visit the site — such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit — to keep the site secure and running, and to show us overall visitor numbers. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors, and we do not sell it. The site itself does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party trackers.

Links to other sites

The site links out to stores such as Best Buy, Home Depot and the PlayStation Store. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them. Please review a store's privacy policy before giving it your information.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. If you have questions about this policy, contact us or email contacts@cadiscount.ca.
