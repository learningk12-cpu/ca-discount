How every deal is checked

A deal only appears on CA Discount if it passes all three checks on the day it is posted. That is what keeps the list trustworthy — and why some big stores have no deal on some days.

  1. 1
    Opened on the store's own page

    The regular price and the sale price are both read on the store's own product page — never from a search snippet, a flyer, or another deals site.

  2. 2
    50% off or more, in Canadian dollars

    The discount is worked out from the two prices on the page. If it is under 50%, or the price is not in CAD on a Canadian store page, it is not listed.

  3. 3
    In stock, with the end date shown

    Stock and offer end dates are noted where the store shows them, so you can see which deals end tonight and which run longer.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how CA Discount works.

Are all deals really 50% off or more?

Yes. Every deal listed is 50% off or more compared with the regular price shown on the store's own product page on the day it was checked. If a discount cannot be confirmed on the store's own page, it is not listed.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes. All prices on CA Discount are in Canadian dollars (CAD), taken from the Canadian store pages.

How often is the site updated?

The deals list is refreshed daily. Each day's page shows the date the prices were checked, and deals come off the list when their offer ends or the discount drops below 50%.

Does CA Discount sell these products?

No. CA Discount lists deals only. Every Shop this deal button takes you to the store's own product page, where you check the final price and buy directly from the store.

Why did a price change when I clicked through?

Stores can change prices or sell out at any time. Prices here were correct when checked on the date shown on each deal — always confirm the price on the store's page before you buy.
