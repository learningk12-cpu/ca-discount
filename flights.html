Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about these fares.

Why are these flights so cheap?

These are January and February dates — the cheapest season to fly to Europe — and most of them are Icelandair through Reykjavík, which undercuts the big carriers on price. Every fare was checked on November 27, 2026.

Why is nonstop so much more expensive?

Nonstop is a convenience premium. On the same dates, Rome nonstop is $1,324.32 against $554.72 with one stop — about $770 more to save roughly 4 hours each way. Paris nonstop is $1,086.82 against $577.72 with one stop.

Are checked bags included?

No. The cheapest fares are the airlines' lightest economy — carry-on only, no changes, no refunds. A fuller fare with one checked bag and changes allowed runs about $150 more on the Icelandair trips.

Can I book a flight on this page?

No. CA Discount lists fares only. Each card links to Google Flights for those exact dates, where you check the final price and book.

What if the price has changed?

Flight fares move fast — sometimes within hours — so always confirm the final price on Google Flights before booking. Every card shows the date its fare was checked.
